# EX.No.-2.2 CREATE A 3D SOLID MODEL FROM ISOMETRIC DRAWING
## Date :

## AIM: 

To create a 3D model from given orthographic projection."
Detailed Explanation: The primary objective of this lab exercise is to convert 2D orthographic engineering drawings—such as the Front View, Top View, and Side View—into a complete, accurate 3D solid model using computer-aided design software.
Through this process, designers learn how to sketch 2D profiles based on structural blueprint and transform them into functional 3D parts using tools like extrusion.

## REQUIREMENTS
1.Autodesk Fusion 360: The core cloud-based Computer-Aided Design (CAD) software utilized for sketching, 3D modeling, and engineering design.
2.Windows 10: The minimum required operating system environment needed to run the Fusion 360 application smoothly and reliably.
3.16 GB of RAM: High-performance memory capacity required to handle complex 3D graphics and rendering operations (integrated graphics with 1 GB VRAM or more is strongly recommended).
4.Internet Connection: A stable broadband connection with a download speed of 2.5 Mbps or faster, and an upload speed of 500 Kbps or faster to support cloud saving and workspace syncing.

## STEP-2:  Select the Sketch Plane
 Open autodesk fusion 360 and choose the TOP(XY)plane to start your initial 2D drawing.

## STEP-3: Create a Sketch 
A. Drawing the Center Rectangle:
1.Select the Tool: Navigate through the menu by clicking Sketch > Rectangle > Center Rectangle.  
2.Find the Origin: Hover your cursor over the origin (the center point) of the sketch area. You should see the cursor automatically snap to this location.  
3.Place the Rectangle: Click once at the origin to begin placing the center rectangle.  
4.Enter Dimensions: Instead of guessing the size, type the Tab button to switch between input fields, and enter 56 mm for the width and 18 mm for the height respectively.  
B. Adding the Arc:
5.Select the Arc Tool: Go to Sketch > Arc > Center point arc.  
6.Position the Arc: Click again to place the value at both ends of the sketch. Your cursor should snap to this location.  
7.Enter Arc Radius: Press the Tab button and enter the value R 18mm for the radius.
C. Adding the Circle and Finishing :
8.Select the Circle Tool: Go to Sketch > circle > Center point circle.  
9.Position the Circle: Click again to place the value on the side of the sketch where it needs to go, making sure the cursor snaps correctly.  
10.Enter Circle Dimensions: Type the Tab button and enter the value dia 16mm for the diameter.  
11.Finalize Placement: Click again to place the sketch element firmly into position.  
12.Finish Sketch: Click the Finish Sketch button to exit the 2D sketching environment and return to the 3D modeling view.

## STEP-4: Extrude Sketch in XY Plane
1.Select Extrude:
  Click on the Extrude tool from the toolbar or menu options to activate the 3D extrusion command.  
2.Select the Sketch:
  Click on the 2D profile/sketch that you drew in the XY plane to highlight and choose it for extrusion.  
3.Click Again to Place the Value:
  Click to confirm or position the extrusion handle/parameter box.  
4.Type in the Value of 8mm:
  Enter 8mm as the height/depth measurement for this base extrusion.  
5.Press ENTER:
  Press the ENTER key on your keyboard to accept the value and finalize the 3D solid extrusion.

## STEP-5:  Start the sketch command
   Action: Select Sketch > Create Sketch.  
Explanation: This command opens up the sketching environment in Fusion 360, allowing you to draw a new 2D profile on top of the 3D model you already created.

## STEP-6: Select the Sketch Plane
Action: Select the TOP SURFACE of the 3D block.  
Explanation: Instead of drawing on the original flat grid (XY plane), you are telling the software to use the top face of your existing 3D solid as the new flat working surface for your next drawing layer.
  
## STEP-7: Create a RECTANGLE 
1.Select the Tool: Navigate to Sketch > RECTANGLE > CENTER RECTANGLE.  
2.Find the Origin: Hover your cursor over the origin (the center point) of the sketch. Your cursor should automatically snap to this location.  
3.Place the Rectangle: Click once at the origin point to begin placing the rectangle.  
4.Enter Dimensions: Press the Tab button on your keyboard and enter 40mm and 10mm for the width and height respectively. (Note: If your local project drawing 5.specifies another dimension like 18mm for height as seen in earlier blocks, ensure you match your assignment guidelines).  
6.Confirm Placement: Click again to place and lock the rectangle into position.  
7.Finish Sketch: Click Finish Sketch to complete this 2D drawing phase.

## STEP-8:  Extrude RECTANGLE 
1.Select Extrude: Click on the Extrude tool from the menu.  
2.Select Sketch: Click on the rectangle sketch you just created to choose it as the profile for extrusion.  
3.Position the Value: Click again to place or activate the extrusion parameter handle.  
4.Enter Measurement: Type in the value of 32mm for the extrusion height.  
5.Accept Value: Press the ENTER key on your keyboard to finalize and create the 3D solid block.

## STEP-9: Create a TRIANGLE 
1.Select the Tool: Click on Sketch > TRIANGLE > CENTER TRIANGLE.  
2.Locate the Origin: Hover your cursor over the origin (the center point) of the sketch until you see the cursor automatically snap to this precise location.  
3.Start Drawing: Click once at the snapped origin to begin placing the triangle.  
4.Enter Dimensions: Press the Tab key to switch between dimension fields, then enter 32mm for the width and 12mm for the height respectively.  
5.Place the Triangle: Click once more to lock the triangle shape onto the sketch plane.  
6.Finish Sketch: Click Finish Sketch to exit the 2D sketch mode.

## STEP-10:  Extrude TRIANGLE
1.Select Extrude Tool: Click on Extrude from the toolbar.  
2.Select the Sketch Profile: Click directly on the TRIANGLE sketch profile you just drew.  
3.Set Position: Click again in the workspace to place or activate the extrusion value prompt.  
4.Set Distance & Direction: Type in the value of 6mm and choose the in both direction (Symmetric) option.  
5.Finalize Extrusion: Press ENTER on your keyboard to accept the value and create the 3D triangular extrusion.

## STEP-11: Create a PENTAGON 
1.Select the Tool: Navigate through the menu by clicking Sketch > polygon > circumscribed polygon.  
2.Locate the Origin: Hover your cursor over the origin (the center point) of the sketch until you see the cursor automatically snap to this precise location.  
3.Start Drawing: Click once at the snapped origin to begin placing the polygon.  
4.Enter Dimensions: Press the Tab button to switch input fields, enter 40mm and 30mm for the width and height respectively, and choose 3 Side (or the required polygon side count based on your design).  
5.Place the Polygon: Click again to place and lock the polygon onto the sketch plane.  
6.Finish Sketch: Click Finish Sketch to exit the 2D sketch mode.

## STEP-12: Extrude PENTAGON
1.Select Extrude Tool: Click on Extrude from the toolbar.  
2.Select the Sketch Profile: Click directly on the PENTAGON sketch profile that you just drew.  
3.Position the Value: Click again in the workspace to place or activate the extrusion parameter handle.  
4.Enter Measurement: Type in the value of 10mm for the extrusion depth.  
5.Accept Value: Press the ENTER key on your keyboard to accept the value and complete the 3D extrusion.

## STEP-13: Create a CIRCLE 
1.Select the Tool: Click on Sketch > CIRCLE > CENTER CIRCLE from the menu bar.  
2.Locate the Origin: Hover your cursor over the origin (Pentagon end side) of the sketch until the cursor automatically snaps to this location.  
3.Start Drawing: Click once at the snapped origin to begin placing the CIRCLE.  
4.Enter Dimensions: Press the Tab button to switch between input fields, then enter DIA 30mm and 20mm for the inner and outer diameters respectively.  
5.Place the Circle: Click again in the canvas area to confirm and place the circle.  
6.Finish Sketch: Click Finish Sketch after completing the 2D drawing phase.

## STEP-14:  Extrude CIRCLE 
1.Select Extrude Tool: Click on the Extrude tool from the 3D menu toolbar.  
2.Select the Sketch Profile: Click directly on the circle sketch region (labeled as pentagon sketch region in the doc).  
3.Position the Value: Click again in the workspace to place or activate the extrusion value prompt.  
4.Enter Measurement & Direction: Type in the value of 10mm on both side to extrude symmetrically in both directions.  
5.Accept Value: Press ENTER on your keyboard to accept the value and finalize the model.
